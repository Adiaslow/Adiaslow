# Adam Murray (@Adiaslow)

PhD student in Biophysics at UCSF (Shoichet Lab), working on computational drug discovery. Previously at UC Santa Cruz, where I worked in the Lokey Lab (cyclic peptide discovery), Kim Lab (neuroscience imaging), and Yarov-Yarovoy Lab at UC Davis (ion channel binder design).

My work sits at the intersection of cheminformatics, geometric deep learning, and category theory, with a focus on finding minimal mathematical structures with maximal expressive power. Current interests:

- Large-library virtual screening and prioritization algorithms
- Molecular fingerprinting and graph representation learning
- Categorical semantics for molecular computation
- Macrocycle design
